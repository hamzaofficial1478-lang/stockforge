How much of the original to keep

Recovery creates an editable master from the image without rewriting or mixing it. Font matches and missing artwork may still need editing. The variation controls below apply only when you choose “Create a new variation”. Save settings before starting the queue.

Borrow from your other designs 0.50

Each ingredient — the palette, the grid, the type pairing, the decoration — is either kept from this listing or taken from another of yours. At 0.40, roughly six in ten stay. At 0 nothing is borrowed and you get this design back.

0 — keep everything from this listing 1 — borrow everything it can
How hard to push it0.50

What happens after the borrowing: colours rotate, type sizes spread apart, margins open or close, the page becomes airier or tighter. Low keeps the piece recognisable; high makes it a different layout in the same family.

0 — leave it alone1 — move everything
How different it must end up 0.70

A gate, not a dial. The result is compared against your listing and must score at least this to be accepted; below it, another round runs. Set this high while asking to keep most of the original and the two fight each other — it will keep pushing the design away and then send it to Review. Lower it whenever you lower the two above.

0 — accept anything 1 — accept only something unrecognisable
How close a motif has to match 0.45

How near a drawing in your library must be before it is placed. Raise it and more designs go to Review telling you what to draw; lower it and you start shipping approximate decoration. A wrong pumpkin ships — a hole you can see does not.

0 — place the nearest thing 1 — only an exact match
Rounds before giving up

Each round borrows and pushes harder than the last. If it still hasn't cleared the gate after this many, the design goes to Review rather than being pushed further.

Artwork it needs and hasn’t got

What this list is. Each row is a piece of decoration the program found in your designs — a bat, a pumpkin, a calendar icon — that it has no drawing for in assets/motifs/.

Why it stopped instead of guessing. It will not trace your original or invent a shape. Tracing produces the jagged, muddy vectors both agencies reject on sight, and a made-up pumpkin is not the one that sold. So it leaves the gap and tells you, which is the only honest answer it has.

What happens next, and it does need you. Nothing on this list clears itself. Someone has to draw each one once, as a plain SVG on a 0–100 square, and drop it in assets/motifs/. Press the button below and the program writes a named, tagged, empty file for each row so you are opening a file rather than working out what to make. Draw into it, press Run again on the designs below, and they carry on from where they stopped.

Why it is worth doing once. The number on the left is how many designs are stuck on that one drawing. Draw the bat once and every design using a bat is unblocked, for good, across the whole catalogue.
